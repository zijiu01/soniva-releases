# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 28, 2026 (Last updated: August 12, 2026)**

Soniva values your personal privacy and data security. This policy explains what information we
collect when you use the Soniva web product, how we use it, whether it's shared with third
parties, and how you can control it.

---

## Information We Collect

**Account information**: When you register/log in with an email, use email-verification-code
login, or log in via Google / Apple third-party sign-in, we store your email address and an
account identifier generated by the login system. Third-party sign-in never provides us with
your Google / Apple password — we only receive the identity credential after a successful login.

**Avatar and nickname**: Avatars you upload are stored in our cloud object storage; profile data
like your nickname lives in the settings record bound to your account.

**Chat and reading session records**: If you're logged in, sessions you create in Soniva and the
text you send/read are synced and stored in the cloud database bound to your account, readable
only by you. Usage when logged out is kept only in your current browser's local storage and is
never uploaded.

**Third-party API keys (BYOK)**: If you fill in your own TTS/translation provider keys in
Settings, they're by default kept only in your current browser's local storage and never sent to
our servers. If you proactively turn on the "Sync to account" toggle, the keys are stored in the
cloud database after encryption, used only to make requests to your designated provider on your
behalf while logged into the same account, never for any other purpose, and never shared with
third parties other than that provider.

**Usage and billing records**: Whether or not you're logged in, we record the character count or
call count consumed by each speech-synthesis/translation request, for calculating your quota
usage and billing. If you're a paid subscriber, your subscription status and payment status are
processed and recorded by the payment provider Paddle (see "Third-Party Providers" below).

**Input text used for product analytics**: By default, we save the full input text of your
speech-reading or translation requests along with usage records, to analyze common usage
scenarios and improve the product. It is never used to train third-party models or sold
externally. You can turn this off anytime under **Settings → Content & Cache → Allow input text
to be recorded for product analytics**; turning it off doesn't affect billing or quota
statistics — it simply stops retaining the original text.

**Information stored on your device**: To keep the product working when logged out, offline, or
when switching accounts, the browser's local storage (localStorage) holds some data, including
theme appearance, language preference, temporary session cache while logged out, and third-party
keys that haven't had cloud sync enabled. This data exists only on your own device and can't be
read by our servers.

---

## How We Use This Information

We use the collected information to: provide core features like speech synthesis/translation,
maintain your account and cross-device sync, calculate subscription quotas and billing, analyze
product usage to improve the experience, and communicate service changes or security matters
with you when necessary. We do not use your personal data or reading/translation text for
purposes unrelated to delivering the service, nor do we sell it to any third party.

---

## Third-Party Providers

To provide the service, we pass necessary information to the following categories of third
parties, limited to the minimum scope required for their functions:

- **Supabase**: our account-system and database provider, hosting storage of account information,
  session records, and avatar files.
- **Paddle**: our payment and subscription processing provider, acting as the Merchant of Record
  for transactions and handling your payment information; our own servers never touch or store
  your card numbers or other payment credentials.
- **Speech-synthesis / translation providers** (Alibaba Cloud, Volcano Engine, MiniMax, Tencent
  Cloud, Baidu Cloud, iFlytek, ElevenLabs, Microsoft Azure, Cartesia, DeepSeek, etc.): only when
  you choose to use a given brand is the text you want read/translated sent to that provider to
  generate the result; system-provided brands route through our servers as a proxy, and with
  your own key (BYOK) that provider is simultaneously your own direct customer relationship.
- **Google / Apple third-party sign-in**: used only for authentication and never reads your
  other data on those platforms.
- **Google Analytics**: used to measure site visits and common usage paths; it writes analytics
  cookies in your browser (see the next section).

---

## Cookies & Local Storage Technologies

Soniva uses two kinds of technology stored in your browser:

1. **localStorage**: used to keep theme, language, account cache, and unsynced keys, as described
   above under "Information stored on your device." This data isn't automatically sent to our
   servers with requests.
2. **Cookies**: we use **Google Analytics** for site traffic statistics; it writes analytics
   cookies in your browser to identify visit sessions. These cookies aren't used for targeted
   advertising, but they are non-essential (analytics) cookies.

The first time you visit Soniva, a cookie-consent prompt appears in the bottom-right corner: only
after you click "Accept" do we load Google Analytics and write analytics cookies; clicking
"Only necessary" won't load them. You can reset this choice anytime by clearing browser data,
without affecting the core reading/translation features.

---

## Data Storage & Security

We use industry-standard encrypted transmission protocols (HTTPS/TLS). Account data has
row-level security (RLS) enabled at the database layer, ensuring only the account owner can read
their own session records, settings, and keys. Third-party API keys you sync to your account are
encrypted server-side before being stored in the database, so the database and ordinary data
access channels can't see the plaintext keys.

Despite the above measures, no method of internet transmission or storage is absolutely secure,
and we can't guarantee 100% security of your data.

---

## Data Retention & Deletion

You can export your session records anytime in account settings, or delete your account and
request deletion of your cloud data (sessions, settings, synced keys, avatar files). After
deletion, cache data left in your local browser from when you were logged out isn't cleared
automatically; you can clear browser data manually.

## Children's Privacy

Soniva's service is aimed at users with full legal capacity. We don't knowingly collect
personal information from minors under 14 years of age (or the age specified by law in your
region); if you find a minor has used the service and submitted personal information without a
guardian's consent, please contact us so we can help handle it.

---

## International Data Transfers

Because some of the providers we use (cloud database, payments, some TTS/translation vendors)
may run servers outside mainland China, your information may be processed across borders during
transmission and storage. We require the relevant providers to follow reasonable data-protection
standards.
